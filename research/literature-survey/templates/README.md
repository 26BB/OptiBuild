## 📐 Key Formatting Specifications (A4 Standard)
- **Page Size:** A4 (210 mm × 297 mm)
- **Columns:** Two columns (width: 88 mm / 3.46 in each; column spacing: 5.1 mm / 0.20 in)
- **Top Margin:** 19 mm (0.75 in)
- **Bottom Margin:** 43 mm (1.69 in)
- **Left / Right Margins:** 14.32 mm (0.56 in)
- **Body Font:** Times New Roman, 10 pt, justified, single line spacing
- **Paper Title:** 24 pt, Bold, Centered
- **Author Names:** 11 pt, Regular, Centered
- **Author Affiliations:** 10 pt, Italic, Centered
- **Abstract & Keywords:** 9 pt, Bold heading, Italic text
- **References:** 8 pt, regular, numbered in square brackets `[1]`
